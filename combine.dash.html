Overview

Statewide snapshot

Six headline metrics spanning both workstreams. Achievement, absenteeism, graduation, student-teacher ratio, and FRL rate are school-level averages (~1,700-1,850 NC public schools). Teacher attrition is the officially published statewide rate (NC DPI, 2024-25) — a district-level figure, not derived from the school-level data.

What this dashboard covers

County Explorer lets you look up any of NC's 100 counties and see how it compares to the state average. Explore Factors lets you pick a risk factor and an outcome and see their relationship directly. Key Insights summarizes the policy-relevant takeaways from both analyses in plain language.
