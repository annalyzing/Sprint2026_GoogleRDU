Absence rate = pct from the 2024–25 school accountability file. Performance rate = spg_grade, the state's School Performance Grade. Attrition = lea_attrition_pct by county.

Source files don't include exact building coordinates, so school points are placed at their reported city (falling back to county center for a small number of records) — locations are accurate to the town, not the parcel.
