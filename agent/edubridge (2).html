Closing the opportunity gap, county by county.

Two students twenty miles apart in North Carolina can have wildly different odds of graduating, simply because of where they live. EduBridge makes that gap visible — and connects families, students, and policymakers to the resources that close it.

Our story

EduBridge started with a simple observation: access to opportunity shouldn't depend on ZIP code. We built EduBridge to make the gap visible and put families, students, and policymakers in front of the resources that close it.

About us

We're a small team of educators, engineers, and former school-board staff. EduBridge combines public data with AI-guided search so every family and district can find what they need in one place.
