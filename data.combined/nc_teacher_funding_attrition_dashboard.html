Attrition vs. graduation outcomes

LEA attrition rate vs. 2025 four-year graduation rate

Stratified sample of 29 districts spanning the full range of attrition (6.5%–40.8%). r = −0.31, R² = 0.094 — a real, moderate correlation, though it falls just short of conventional significance at this sample size.
Line of best fit: Graduation rate = 91.07 − 0.158 × (Attrition rate)
How to read the two correlations together: county wealth only weakly predicts a district's attrition rate (r = −0.12 to +0.19), but attrition rate more directly predicts graduation outcomes (r = −0.31). That means funding matters mainly through what it buys — the ability to recruit and keep experienced teachers — not as a direct, one-step driver of student outcomes on its own.
